Lo and Behold - The Game

In this 2.5d stylized adventure, you get to uncover the mysteries that have disrupted the natural circle of life.

An ancient evil lurks on the underground, a vulture called Blightfeather, the Vulture King. Your objective is to go through forests, swamps and caverns defeating creatures and bosses to gather enough power to end Blightfeather's reign and restore the balance.

While not deep into dungeons, Lo hangs about on a beautiful and immersive animal village where he can find important NPCs that can unlock new abilities, grant boons or even make the dungeons harder while improving the rewards he gets in dungeons.
